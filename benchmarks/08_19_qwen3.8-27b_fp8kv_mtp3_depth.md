## Observations

- Prefill and e2e TTFT reproduce the morning patched sweep to within ~1%
  (2827/2765/2701/2583/2366/2034 vs 2797/2757/2704/2591/2371/2036 t/s; TTFT
  2.17/3.71/6.83/13.48/28.58/63.95 vs 2.20/3.72/6.82/13.44/28.51/63.90 s) —
  no drift from the d0 run, no regression from the patches.
- Decode is noisier: 53–60 t/s through d32K (vs 60–62 in the morning), 50.1 at
  d64K, 41.2 at d128K (−32% vs d8K). The morning sweep's d64K dip (45.7) is
  mirrored here, so d64K is consistently the weakest point; d128K spread across
  the two sweeps (41–48) reflects ~2-run-sample variance on tg32, which is
  already small (±2-7 t/s). Net: decode decays with depth as expected; the
  patched build introduces no throughput regression.
- Coherence passed on all six depths — no MTP token loops, garbage, or NaN
  corruption on the hybrid GDN path.
