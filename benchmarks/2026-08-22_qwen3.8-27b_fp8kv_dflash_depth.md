# 2026-08-22 Qwen3.8-27B — depth & concurrency-at-depth (calibrated fp8 KV, DFlash)

Post-calibration re-measure of the default `qwen3.8-27b` profile (calibrated
q/k/v fp8 KV scales + DFlash2 drafter). Protocol matches the earlier
08_19 depth sweep: llama-benchy `--tg 32 --depth D --runs 2 --no-cache`
(full prefill of `depth + 2048` per run, no prefix caching), thinking off,
`--max-num-seqs 2`. Stack: vLLM v0.28.0rc2, torch 2.13, AITER v0.1.20,
ROCM_AITER_UNIFIED_ATTN, 2× R9700.

## Observations

- **Decode decays hard with depth under DFlash.** tg32 falls 60.9 (d4K) →
  37.5 (d32K) → 24.4 (d64K) → 14.8 (d128K). The 08_19 MTP3 depth sweep held
  decode far better at depth (MTP3 d128K ≈ 41 t/s vs DFlash ≈ 15), so DFlash2's
  decode win is short-context only; at long context it loses badly to MTP.
- **Concurrency 2 at depth hurts total decode throughput.** Total tg32 drops
  from 36.4 (c1) to 12.9 (c2) at d16384, and collapses to 2.2 (c2) at d65536
  vs 23.4 (c1). For DFlash at depth, concurrency 1 is strongly preferred
  (the #35288 cap keeps `max-num-seqs` at 2, but these results argue for
  serving deep-context requests at low concurrency).
- **DFlash decode at depth is very noisy** (d16384 tg32: 58 in one sweep,
  36 in another, ±14 std). Treat absolute decode figures at moderate depth as
  indicative, not precise.
- Prefill (pp2048) is stable and matches expectations: 2775 → 2031 t/s over
  the depth range; e2e TTFT scales linearly with depth (2.2 → 64 s), no
  regression from the KV-scale calibration.
