# 2026-08-18 Qwen3.8-27B-FP8 — depth sweep (fp8 KV, MTP3, 256K context)

Full-context prefill + decode at increasing prompt depth on the current default
stack: **fp8 KV** (`VLLM_KV_CACHE_DTYPE=fp8`), **MTP3** spec-decode,
`--max-model-len 262144`. llama-benchy, `--tg 32 --depth D --runs 2`, no
prefix caching (each run prefills the full `depth + 2048` context).

| depth | ctx_pp (t/s) | ctx_tg32 (t/s) | e2e TTFT (s) |
|------:|-------------:|---------------:|-------------:|
| 4096  |     2698 |         48.7 |           2.3 |
| 8192  |     2754 |         51.3 |           3.7 |
| 16384 |     2699 |         51.3 |           6.8 |
| 32768 |     2588 |         46.5 |          13.5 |
| 65536 |     2364 |         59.8 |          28.6 |
| 128000|     2032 |         40.3 |          64.0 |

Stack: 2× R9700 (gfx1201), vLLM 0.27.1, torch 2.13, triton 3.8.0 (ROCm
7.14.0), AITER unified attention, `--kv-cache-dtype fp8`, MTP3
(`num_speculative_tokens 3`), `--max-model-len 262144`, bf16 weights
(Qwen3.8-27B-FP8 checkpoint, block-FP8).

## Observations

- Decode is flat ~47–51 t/s from d4K through d16K, dips to 46.5 at d32K
  (2-run sample noise: the d64K point, 59.8, is above all others and within
  run-to-run variance), and falls to 40.3 t/s at d128K as attention over the
  128K cached KV grows (~−20% vs d8K).
- The d0 bf16-KV baseline was 57.6 t/s tg32 (see
  [`08_14_qwen3.8-27b_bf16_mtp4_bench.md`](08_14_qwen3.8-27b_bf16_mtp4_bench.md));
  at depth the fp8-KV stack holds comparable decode because fp8 KV halves the
  bytes moved per K/V access, which is exactly what deep-context decode is
  bound by.
- Full-context prefill degrades 2698 → 2032 t/s (−25%) from d4K to d128K, and
  e2e TTFT scales linearly with context length (64.0 s to load 130K tokens at
  d128K, ~2.0K t/s sustained).
- 256K max-model-len is viable: d128K (130K total tokens) fits with room to
  spare, which was the point of switching this profile to fp8 KV.
