## Takeaways

- **Decode holds through the whole context window**: tg32 sits 41–60 t/s at
  all depths, with no collapse past d64K (41.4 @d128K, 45.7 @d200K,
  47.5 @d256K). Matches the historical fp8-KV shape; no regression from
  `--no-async-scheduling`.
- **Deep prefill/TTFT is fp8-favored**: pp256K 1277 t/s / TTFT 202 s here, vs
  876–953 t/s / 270–294 s on the bf16-KV sweeps (08-22/08-23) — fp8 halves the
  bytes moved per K/V access. The bf16-vs-fp8 depth tradeoff is unchanged from
  `2026-08-22_qwen3.8-27b_depth_bf16_fp8.md`.
- **Coherence passed at every depth** (no NaN, no MTP token loops) — the
  async-off path is stable under full-context prefill + MTP3.
