fused_moe configs, froggeric v21.3 chat template) but at **concurrency 2**.

**Observations:**
- c2 aggregate decode ≈ c4 at mid/deep depth (geomean 86.1 vs 86.0) — i.e. 2
  requests extract almost no extra aggregate throughput over 4, confirming the
  bottleneck is the large-KV decode step itself, not request count.
- c2 e2e TTFT is far better than c4 once contexts get long (1555 ms vs 23488 ms
  @ d64000): with 2 requests, prefill of the 64k context no longer serializes
  against a second competing deep prefill within the 4096-token budget.
- vs c1, c2 aggregate is ~23-68% *lower* per request (per-request t/s @ d64000
  58 vs 181) — the price of any concurrency at extreme depth.
- For interactive agents, c2 is a reasonable middle ground: near-c4 aggregate,
  but ~15× better latency than c4 at 64k contexts.
