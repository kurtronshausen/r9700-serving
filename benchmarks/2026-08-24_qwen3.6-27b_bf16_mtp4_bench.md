pp2048/TTFT regressed (~-30%): the pre-patch baseline ran a different
attention/cache stack, and bf16 KV (current default) doubles K/V bytes per
prefill step vs the fp8-KV era. Decode holds steady (tg32 flat, tg128 +9%).

128K max-model-len scheduling window. No config change was needed for this
profile (MTP4 already inherited from `env/qwen3.6.env.common`); this run
re-validates it on the current build.
